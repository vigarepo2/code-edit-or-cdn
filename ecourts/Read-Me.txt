WIKIO — SVG IDENTITY SET

Design: a custom continuous W with folded coral faces, soft inner joins,
slanted terminals and a slightly raised right tip. The contour is identical
throughout the set. The finish takes its warm palette and restrained light
from the supplied eCourts artwork; the monogram geometry was drawn anew.

USE
Keep the original proportions. Use monochrome below 32px wide; 24px or larger
is preferred, while 16px is a constrained favicon use. Leave free space around
the symbol of at least one main-stroke width. Avoid shadows or animation on
small permanent watermarks. If a low-opacity watermark is wanted, adjust the
opacity of the complete mark rather than the individual faces.

Select the light/dark file according to the actual surface behind the mark.
There is no hidden colour auto-detection, JavaScript, bitmap or external URL.
All text in delivered SVGs is outlined. Letter outlines derive from Nimbus
Sans Bold; the icon is hand-drawn vector geometry. No font files are required.
Every decorative face is clipped to the same primary silhouette. Native
layers have named IDs so a vector editor can change colours and surface finish.

When inserting the same SVG source multiple times INLINE in one HTML document,
prefix its IDs per instance; ordinary separate image-file use needs no change.
Android VectorDrawable does not support every SVG feature directly: use an SVG
renderer, convert through your asset pipeline, or rasterize the app tile.
